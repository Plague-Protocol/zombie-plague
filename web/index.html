How a night goes

Three districts. One clock.

Every chapter is a district with jobs to do before the way out opens. Leave a district with time to spare and it pays, in cash for the stores ahead.

01 · Downtown

Where it started

Scavenge, help who you can and find the parts you need, then get down into the subway before the streets close in.

02 · The docks

Across the tracks

Work through the yards to the gate in the army barricade. Cars and bikes get you there faster, but every engine is heard.

03 · The edge

The last helicopter

The landing zone is the only way out of Port Halden. Get there before the infection reaches zero.

What is out there

The city plays back

☣

The clock is your infection

Every bite jumps it forward. Antiviral doses hold it back. When it runs out, you are one of them.

🦷

Survivors, and who is bitten

People ask for help and some will follow you. Someone coughing may already be bitten: check them, and decide whether to leave them.

🔦

Light and noise

Your light shows you the street and shows you to them. Kill it, throw a bottle to pull them away, and listen for screamers.

❌

Houses and stores

Search houses for doses, cash and rounds, but a red X means something is inside. Spend what you find at weapon stores.

🚗

Cars and bikes

Faster than running, with seats for the people you saved. Watch the fuel, and shake off what grabs on.

👻

Your last self

Where you fell, you wait, still carrying what you carried. Put it down to take your gear back.

Multiplayer

Not everyone gets out

The same streets, with others in them. The server runs the round, so what happens is what happened.

  • Quick play. Drop into the next open room with whoever is around.
  • Private room. Make a room and read out its five-letter code, or type a friend's code to join.
  • Invite link. Send it on WhatsApp or anywhere else and it opens straight into your room.

Sign in to play with others. A round starts a few seconds after four are in, up to eight.

Rooms count too

Every round you play signed in goes on the rooms board: who got out, who turned, who put the most of them down.
